Venue & Travel

Getting to Yeditepe University and making the most of Istanbul

Conference Venue

Yeditepe University

Yeditepe University is located in Kayışdağı, Ataşehir — on the Asian side of Istanbul. The campus is modern, well-equipped, and easily accessible from both sides of the city.

The name Yeditepe (Seven Hills) echoes Istanbul's ancient identity as the "city on seven hills" — a fitting home for a conference that brings together communities from across the country.

Getting There

Istanbul is well connected — here are your main options

✈ By Air

Istanbul has two major airports:

  • Istanbul Airport (IST) — European side. Take a bus (IETT/Havaist) or taxi to the Asian side via the Bosphorus bridges.
  • Sabiha Gökçen Airport (SAW) — Asian side. Much closer to Yeditepe University. Shuttle buses and taxis available.

🚇 By Metro & Public Transport

  • Take the M4 Metro line to Saygıdeğer Ömer Halisdemir station (Ataşehir area)
  • Metrobus connections available from the European side to the Asian side
  • The university runs shuttle services from certain points — details to be announced

🚌 By Bus

Various IETT bus lines serve the Ataşehir and Kayışdağı area. The university stop is well served from central Kadıköy and Üsküdar.

🚖 By Taxi / Ride-Share

Taxis and ride-sharing services (BiTaksi, Uber) are widely available across Istanbul. From Kadıköy, the journey to Yeditepe University takes approximately 20–30 minutes depending on traffic.

🚗 By Car

The campus is located near the D-100 highway. Parking is available on campus. Participants travelling by car are advised to follow navigation to Yeditepe Üniversitesi, Ataşehir.

Accommodation

We recommend staying in these convenient areas

Kadıköy

~25 min to campus

Vibrant neighbourhood on the Asian side with excellent transport links, restaurants, and cultural life. A great base for the conference.

Ataşehir

~10–15 min to campus

The closest major district to Yeditepe University. Modern hotels, shopping, and convenient for commuting to the campus.

Üsküdar

~30 min to campus

A historic and scenic district on the Asian side, with ferry connections to the European side and a range of accommodation options.
