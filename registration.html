Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to submit a paper to attend?

No. You are welcome to attend as a participant without presenting. General and student registration categories are open to all.

Can I attend only one day?

We encourage full two-day attendance. Single-day registration options may be available — please contact the organising committee for details.

How do I pay the registration fee?

Payment is by bank transfer. Full bank details are provided in the registration form itself once you begin registering. Registration fees are 1,200 TL for General Participants and Presenters, and 500 TL for External Students (valid student ID required). Your registration is confirmed once payment is verified, typically within 5 business days.

I am a Yeditepe University staff member. Is there a different process?

Please contact the organising committee directly for internal registration procedures.
