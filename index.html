National Conference

Where research meets the classroom

ELT Conference 2026 brings together researchers, teacher educators, university instructors, and K–12 teachers from across Türkiye for two days of dialogue, discovery, and professional community-building.

Hosted by the English Language Teaching Program at Yeditepe University, the conference is designed as a balanced platform where academic inquiry and classroom practice speak to one another — and to each other.

Plenary Speakers

Three distinguished scholars and practitioners — two on Day 1, one on Day 2

Day 1 · Opening Plenary

Professor Simon Borg

Educational Consultant
ELT & Language Teacher Education

Researcher and consultant with over 35 years in ELT, specialising in language teacher education and professional development across more than 30 countries.

Day 1 · Plenary

Prof. Zeynep Çamlıbel-Acar

Assistant Professor
Marmara University

Specialist in ELT to young learners and language teacher education, with research focusing on parental involvement and extramural English.

Day 2 · Plenary

Professor Hossein Farhady

Assessment & Research Consultant
Former Faculty, Yeditepe University

Applied linguist with over four decades of experience in language testing and assessment, author of some 50 articles and 10 textbooks in the field.
