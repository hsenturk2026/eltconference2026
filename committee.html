The Scientific Committee is responsible for the double-blind peer review of all submitted abstracts and for maintaining the academic standards of the conference programme. Members are drawn from ELT and Applied Linguistics programmes across Türkiye.

Interested in joining the Scientific Committee?

We are currently forming our Scientific Committee and welcome expressions of interest from ELT and Applied Linguistics academics based in Türkiye. Reviewers are expected to evaluate 2–3 abstracts.
